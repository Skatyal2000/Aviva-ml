1. Referral / Assignment Submission
Used to create a new subrogation case. Typical fields include:

Claim Number
Insured Name
Loss Date
Jurisdiction/State
Carrier Information
Adverse Party Information
Cause of Loss
Recovery Potential
Claim Amount / Damages
Supporting Documents and Attachments

Veritas describes this as the process for submitting subrogation assignments and pursuing recovery opportunities. [veritasclaims.com], [subroreferrals.com]
2. Case Summary
Provides a high-level view of the recovery file.
Typical fields:

Assignment Status
Recovery Status
Current Recovery Amount
Assigned Specialist
Key Dates
Liability Determination
Recovery Potential Rating

Veritas states that each assignment is managed by a Subrogation and Recovery Specialist with ongoing case management and reporting. [veritasclaims.com], [veritasclaims.com]
3. Party Information
Tracks all involved entities.
Typical fields:

Insured Information
Claimant Information
Adverse Carrier
Adverse Driver/Owner
Attorney Information
Contact Details

The subrogation process involves coordination with adverse carriers and multiple parties. [veritasclaims.com], [veritasclaims.com]
4. Financial / Recovery Details
Tracks amounts being pursued and recovered.
Typical fields:

Total Claim Paid
Deductible Amount
Recovery Demand Amount
Settlement Amount
Recovered Amount
Outstanding Balance
Recovery Percentage

Veritas emphasizes recovery opportunity review, negotiation, and recovery management throughout the claim lifecycle. [veritasclaims.com], [veritasclaims.com]
5. Document Repository
Stores claim-related evidence.
Typical fields or document categories:

Police Reports
Photos
Repair Estimates
Invoices
Demand Letters
Settlement Agreements
Correspondence

Veritas highlights meticulous documentation and file review as key components of its recovery process. [veritasclaims.com], [veritasclaims.com]
6. Notes & Activity Log
Tracks case actions and communications.
Typical fields:

Date/Time
User
Activity Type
Negotiation Notes
Status Updates
Follow-up Tasks

Veritas references proactive communication and transparent reporting throughout recovery efforts. [veritasclaims.com]
7. Reporting / Dashboard
Management view of recovery performance.
Typical metrics:

Open Assignments
Closed Assignments
Recovery Rate
Recovery Dollars Collected
Aging Reports
Specialist Workload
Pipeline Reports
